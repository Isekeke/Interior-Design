Methods

01

Consultation

Every project begins with a conversation to understand your vision, lifestyle, and the story you want your space to tell.

02

Concept & 2D

We translate ideas into technical blueprints, creating precise 2D layouts that define the flow and architecture of the space.

03

3D Visualization

Experience your home before it exists. Our photorealistic 3D renders bring textures, light, and furniture to life.

04

Procurement

We source the finest materials and bespoke furniture pieces, managing every detail from artisan workshops to your doorstep.

05

The Reveal

The final chapter where design becomes reality. We oversee the installation to ensure every detail is perfect for the reveal.

Our Services

Home Staging Consultation

We work around your schedule. Consultations can be done either at the property in person or via virtual call. Our designers will get a sense of the layout and go over appropriate design strategies.

Color Consultations

All of our stages come complete with both large and small pieces, furniture, art, and full decor. Everything is handpicked and placed in the property. Our team will walk you through recommended design strategies and offer staging advice.

Photography

No need to spend time and money looking for another vendor. We offer complimentary professional photography with every stage.And, photos are typically taken the following business day.
